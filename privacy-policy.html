Privacy Policy — ThermalCast

Effective date: 2026-10-26　|　Last updated: 2026-10-26

1. Overview

ThermalCast (package name com.thermalcast.app) is a weather and variometer tool for paragliding enthusiasts. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the rights you have. The app does not require registration or login, and we never sell your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Precise location: used to fetch weather forecasts for your selected launch site, and to compute pressure altitude and flight analysis. Location data is used on-device to request weather from the weather provider and is not used for advertising or tracking.
  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes to import/export site data. We do not store, upload, or share any images captured through the camera.
  • Device and usage data: if analytics is enabled, we collect anonymous crash and usage statistics to improve stability and the overall experience. This data does not contain permanent identifiers used for ad targeting.
  • Account information: the app has no registration/login flow, so we collect no account, name, or contact details unless you contact us by email.

3. How We Use Information

  • To request and return weather forecasts from the weather data provider for your location / selected site;
  • To compute altitude, pressure altitude, and flight scores locally on your device;
  • To manage and display your saved launch sites (stored locally on the device only);
  • To improve the product through anonymous statistics.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with advertisers. We only send data to the following service providers as necessary to deliver functionality:

  • Open-Meteo (weather data): to obtain forecasts, the requested coordinates are sent to this service. Its privacy and data policy is at open-meteo.com.
  • OpenFreeMap / MapLibre (map tiles): used for offline / online map display.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your saved sites, flight logs, and similar data are stored in a local on-device database and are not automatically uploaded to our servers. Location information is used only when initiating a weather request. We take reasonable measures to protect local data, but please understand that no internet transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

  • You may uninstall the app at any time to remove all local data on the device;
  • If you have any questions or requests about how we handle data, you can reach us using the contact details below.

7. Children

This app is intended for adult paragliding users and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with the effective date noted.
